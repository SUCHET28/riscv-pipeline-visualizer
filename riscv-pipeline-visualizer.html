RISC-V pipeline visualizer

Write a few instructions, then step through a 5-stage pipeline one clock cycle at a time. Stalls, forwarding and branch flushes show up in the timing diagram as they happen.

Program

Supports add, sub, and, or, addi, lw, sw, beq, bne, nop, and labels.

Registers

Hatched cell: stalled. Grey ✕: flushed after a taken branch. ⇢ on EX: operand forwarded.
